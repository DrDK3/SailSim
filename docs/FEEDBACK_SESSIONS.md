# Focused feedback sessions

Design handover of 23 Nov 2026, item 31. A kit for small, task-specific sessions with club or class sailors,
instructors or learners. **Outreach is the owner's**: nobody is contacted on the project's behalf without the owner's
authorisation, and no personal data is collected (under-18 safeguards apply to any junior session: a parent or the
club's safeguarding lead present, no names recorded, nothing filmed).

## Before a session

1. **One task, written so anyone can repeat it**: the build (the commit or the site's date), the device, the start
   (for example "Learn map → lesson 1, Learn level, default settings"), and what the person is asked to do.
2. **What we want to learn**, as one or two questions (for example "Can a new player say what the lesson tick means?").
3. **The evidence kind** it can give (docs/CURRENT_SPEC.md → Evidence words): sailor review, learning evidence or a
   measurement. A session never turns a software check into validation.

## During

- Observe; do not coach unless the task is stuck for two minutes.
- Note what was confusing, what failed repeatedly, and what was enjoyed, **separately** (item 10's protocol).
- Note why someone chose to repeat (Race it again, Try again), in their words if they offer them.

## After: the log (one row per finding)

| Date | Build | Task | Who (role only) | Finding | Kind | Disposition | Link |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| (yyyy-mm-dd) | (commit) | (task id) | (for example "club helm, 10 years") | (what happened) | confusion / repeated failure / enjoyment / rules / realism | fixed in (commit) / issue (number) / no change (why) | (issue or pull request) |

A finding is closed only with a disposition. Changes made because of a finding say so in their pull request, and the
round report lists them.

## Suggested first sessions (from the design handover)

| Task | Item | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Lesson 1 to its result card, skipping one step; then explain the tick | 3, 7 | new players |
| A race, then Race it again: understand the result and the comparison | 6, 10 | club racers |
| Rules incidents in the protest room: are the explanations right? | 4 | a qualified rules reviewer |
| Tack and recover with the acknowledgement on: what earned it? | 5, 11 | an instructor and learners |
| The challenge link sent through a messaging app, opened on a phone | 24 | two players |
| Back up progress, restore in another browser | 25 | any player |
| The device check on phones, tablets and a real GPU | 12 | volunteers with devices |
