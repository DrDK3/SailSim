# SailSim's club table: a note for parents (DRAFT)

**Draft for the owner to check before use.** It describes the app as built. It is not a legal notice.

SailSim is a sailing simulator that runs in a web browser. It is free, and nobody needs an account. Most of it never
sends anything anywhere.

**The weekly course.** Each week there is one course with the same wind for everyone. Your child can sail it as often
as they like. Their best times are kept only on their own device.

**Ghosts and challenges.** After a race, your child can make a link to send to a friend. The link holds their run and
a made-up name, such as "Blue Heron 42". Names are picked from fixed word lists; nobody can type a name. When the
friend opens the link, they race a "ghost": a recording of how your child sailed. It cannot react or chat. Nothing is
uploaded: the link itself carries the run.

**The club table** (only if the club switches it on). After sailing the weekly course, your child may press "Submit to
the club table". The app explains first what is sent:

- how they sailed the course;
- their made-up name;
- which version of the app and which kind of browser they used;
- a random code kept on their device, so they can remove the entry later.

It sends nothing else: no real name, no email, no account, no location. The simulator checks each run automatically.
The table shows only the made-up name, the time, whether it was sailed with or without help, and the date. Runs are
deleted after checking, except the week's three fastest, which others can race as ghosts. Everything is removed at the
end of the season. Your child can press "Remove my entry" at any time, and it goes within about half an hour.

**What the app does not do.** It has no chat, no messages, no friend lists, no notifications, no daily streaks, no
rewards for coming back, no adverts and no tracking.
